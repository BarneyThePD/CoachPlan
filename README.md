## Coaching principles

- **60 minutes maximum for a treadmill running session**, including the usual 5-minute walking warm-up; cooldown can be separate.
- One genuinely hard running session per week **initially**, plus an easy aerobic session and an optional controlled endurance/parkrun day, depending on recovery.
- Easy means conversational and genuinely easy; **do not turn every aerobic session into a hard progression**.
- Keep 1–2 strength sessions weekly, scaled around leg soreness and running.
- Stop or modify when lower-leg/first-step foot pain, fatigue, poor sleep or illness makes load inappropriate. No fixed HR cutoff substitutes for symptoms and effort.
- Prefer consistent training and fresh 5k/10k benchmarks over repeated speculative fitness estimates.
