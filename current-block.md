## Weekly structure once recovered

- **One quality run** (13.0–13.5 intervals *or* threshold work, not both as maximal tests).
- **One easy aerobic run**, preferably conversational throughout rather than a ladder to HR 190+.
- **One optional controlled endurance run or parkrun**, depending on recovery and family schedule.
- **1–2 strength sessions**, maintaining running-supportive lower body and progressive upper body, with leg loading adjusted to soreness.
- Rest days between demanding sessions. Do not make every run a performance test.

## Decision rules

1. Symptoms and perceived effort take precedence over an arbitrary treadmill speed or HR target.
2. If lower-leg/foot pain appears or increases during running, stop impact activity.
3. Fatigue and poor sleep may require a lighter week even if a recent session was a milestone.
4. Maintain the **60-minute running cap** and allow the training programme to adapt to work/family commitments.
5. Review goals after measured fresh efforts rather than overfitting estimates to interval HR.
