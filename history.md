# Training History

This file records the training journey that led into the current structured sub-50 10k block.

## Background

Robert began structured endurance training in January 2026 with limited recent running background. The first major target was the Edinburgh Marathon on 24 May 2026.

### Athlete context

- Height: approximately 183 cm
- Weight: approximately 82.8 kg by late May 2026, down from about 89 kg in January
- Family and work commitments make long weekend sessions difficult
- Most structured training is completed on a treadmill before work

---

## Edinburgh Marathon block

### Training pattern

- Tuesday: aerobic
- Thursday: tempo
- Sunday: long run

### Key build-up marker

- 3 May 2026: 150-minute long run, 20.19 km

### Supporting 5k markers

- 25 April 2026: parkrun in 29:59 on a hilly course
- 2 May 2026: parkrun in 29:48

### 24 May 2026 — Edinburgh Marathon

- Finish: **5:38:37**
- Conditions: approximately 23°C and 60% humidity
- Target had been sub-4
- Heat was a major limiting factor
- Post-race issues included right-leg pain, shivering and significant sunburn

### Coaching interpretation

The marathon result did not reflect the underlying training fitness. The conditions and heat response were the dominant factors. The immediate priority became recovery, rebuilding consistency and shifting toward shorter-distance goals.

---

## Post-marathon transition

### New goals at the start of the block

- Primary: sub-50 10k
- Secondary: sub-27 5k
- Longer term: sub-45 10k, Serpentine 10 km swim in September 2027, sub-3:30 marathon attempt in March 2028

### Goal revision — 3 August 2026

The Serpentine 10 km swim was moved to a future/on-hold goal because the required swim-specific training time was unlikely to be available over the following year.

The replacement primary 2027 target became **sub-20 5k**, with the understanding that this is a long-range ambition requiring intermediate milestones such as sub-25, sub-23 and sub-21, plus a substantial increase in running durability and economy.

### Emerging weekly structure

- One quality session
- One aerobic progression
- One parkrun or equivalent

The programme deliberately prioritised consistency and controlled progression over aggressive speed increases.
