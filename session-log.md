## 4 September 2026 — Clean aerobic comparison + 12.5 touch + press progression

**Run**

- 5 min walk @ 6.5 km/h
- 10 min @ 8.5 km/h — HR **153**
- 10 min @ 9.5 km/h — HR **169**
- 5 min @ 10.5 km/h — HR **176** at 30:00
- Continued @ 10.5 to 35:00 — HR **178**
- 5 min @ 11.0 km/h — HR **187** at 40:00
- Continued @ 11.0 to 45:00 — HR remained **187**
- 3 min @ 12.5 km/h — HR **194** at 48:00
- 2 min @ 8.5 km/h — HR **185** at 50:00

**Total**

- **50:00**
- **8.01 km**
- 5 min cooldown walk completed afterwards

**Assessment**

This was the first clean like-for-like post-Cornwall aerobic comparison. Early-session HR remained approximately 12–15 bpm above the stronger pre-holiday readings at 8.5–9.5 km/h, so the planned first 13.0 km/h exposure was appropriately withheld. HR response settled somewhat later in the progression: 187 remained stable across the latter 5 minutes of the 11.0 block. A single 3-minute 12.5 exposure was completed, reaching HR 194, after which quality work stopped. Current interpretation: speed capacity remains accessible, but aerobic efficiency/readiness has not yet fully returned to the pre-holiday baseline. Continue normal training and reassess over the next few sessions rather than forcing faster progression.

**Lateral incline press**

- Set 1 — **10 reps @ 10 kg per side** (10th rep was accidental but completed)
- Set 2 — **9 reps @ 10 kg per side**; a 10th would likely have been a struggle
- Set 3 — **9 reps @ 10 kg per side**

**Strength assessment**

Clear progressive overload from 31 August: **8/8/8 → 10/9/9 at the same 10 kg-per-side load**, increasing total reps from 24 to 28 (+16.7%). Keep the load unchanged and continue progressing toward 3 × 12 before adding resistance. No additional strength work was performed after the press.
